**The trigger**: On Nov 9, 2020, Pfizer announced its COVID vaccine trial data showed over 90% efficacy — way better than anyone expected. This was the first real proof a vaccine could work, after 8 months of pandemic uncertainty. Markets reacted violently the next day (Nov 10).

**Why hotel REITs specifically exploded**:

1. **They were the most beaten-down group** — hotels were down ~39% from pre-COVID levels even after months of recovery. Empty hotels, no business travel, no tourism = these stocks had been left for dead.
2. **Vaccine = reopening = travel comes back**. The entire investment thesis for hotel REITs (Pebblebrook, Ryman, Park Hotels) is simple: people need to physically travel and stay in rooms for these companies to make money. A vaccine directly attacks the #1 reason nobody was traveling.
3. **This is a "reopening trade" / rotation move**, not a fundamental earnings beat like your REZI example. Nothing about Pebblebrook's actual Q3 numbers changed on Nov 10 — the stock moved purely on _future expectations_ repricing overnight.
4. **Bank of America's "more room to run" call** matters because it's forward-looking, not backward-looking. They were saying: even after this vaccine pop, these stocks are still cheap relative to where they'll be once travel actually normalizes. That's an analyst validating that the rally isn't overextended yet — 39% below pre-COVID with a vaccine now real means there's a gap left to close.
