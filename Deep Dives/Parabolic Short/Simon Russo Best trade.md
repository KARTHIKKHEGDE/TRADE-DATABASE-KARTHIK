```
DWAC PARABOLIC SHORT — RECONSTRUCTED PROCESS

1. INITIAL SHORT
   Entry: $160
   Stop: $200
   Risk: $1,000,000

   Position size:
   $1,000,000 ÷ ($200 - $160)
   = 25,000 shares short

2. FIRST COVER / PROFIT REALIZATION
   Price collapses toward ~$90.

   Hypothesis: cover 50% of the position at ~$90.

   Shares covered:
   25,000 ÷ 2 = 12,500 shares

   Profit realized:
   12,500 × ($160 - $90)
   = $875,000

   Remaining short:
   12,500 shares

3. PRICE BOUNCES BACK TOWARD VWAP
   Assume VWAP ≈ $120.

   Original risk unit: $1,000,000
   Realized profit: $875,000

   Reusable risk pool:
   $1,000,000 + $875,000
   = $1,875,000

4. ADD AT VWAP
   Add price: ~$120
   New stop: ~$140

   Risk per added share:
   $140 - $120 = $20

   Add size:
   $1,875,000 ÷ $20
   = 93,750 shares

   Therefore:
   Existing short = 12,500 shares
   New add = 93,750 shares

   Total short position:
   106,250 shares

5. EXIT / COVER
   The added position is then covered as the price moves down toward the
   65 EMA / designated mean-reversion target.

   Exact profit depends on the actual 65 EMA exit price.

6. NEXT MORNING GAP-UP
   If the stock gaps back up, the process can use the newly realized
   profit as additional risk capital for the next short/add.

   For any new short:
   
   Position size =
   Risk allocated ÷ (Stop price - Entry price)
```
