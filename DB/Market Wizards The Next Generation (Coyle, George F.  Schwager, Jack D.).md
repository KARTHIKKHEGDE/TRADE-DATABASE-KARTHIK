- A **parabolic move** is when a stock accelerates upward extremely fast, making increasingly larger moves (for example, going from $1 to $7 in a few days); at this stage, the **all-time high of the run becomes the key level** because the short thesis is essentially that the stock can no longer make new highs. When the stock fails to break that high and produces its **first meaningful red day** (around -10% or more for an extended small-cap), it can indicate that momentum has broken and the stock may unwind rapidly; the trader can then consider a short with the **stop above the parabolic high**, because a break above that high would invalidate the thesis.
