- **20% Up Stocks > 100** = market is very strong, breakouts work well.
- **20% Up Stocks > 150** = market may be overheated.
- After such spikes, a **pullback often comes within 3–5 days**.
- Use this as a **market breadth indicator** to judge whether conditions are favorable for breakout trading or whether risk is increasing.
